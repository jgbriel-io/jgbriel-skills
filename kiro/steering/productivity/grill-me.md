# Grill Me

Interview the user relentlessly about every aspect of their plan until reaching shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies one-by-one. Provide a recommended answer for each question.

Ask one question at a time. If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore instead.
